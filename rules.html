Server Rules

Please follow these rules to keep the server fun, fair, and safe for everyone.

1

No Hacking, Cheats, or Exploits

Using hacked clients, X-Ray packs, auto-clickers, or exploiting bugs/glitches will result in a permanent ban. Report bugs to staff instead!

2

Respect All Players

No racism, sexism, harassment, toxic behavior, or excessive swearing. Keep the chat friendly and clean.

3

No Unauthorized Advertising

Promoting other servers, Discords, or external links without staff permission is strictly prohibited.

4

Protect Personal Privacy

Do not share sensitive personal information (phone numbers, real-life pictures, etc.) of yourself or others.

5

Staff Authority

Moderator and Admin decisions are final. Respect staff instructions and do not argue about timeouts or punishments.
