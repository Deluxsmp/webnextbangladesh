Server Rules

Please follow these rules carefully to keep the server fun, fair, and safe for everyone.

01

No Hacking, Cheats, or Exploits

Using hacked clients, X-Ray packs, auto-clickers, or exploiting bugs/glitches will result in a permanent ban. Report bugs to staff instead!

02

Respect All Players

No racism, sexism, harassment, toxic behavior, or excessive swearing. Keep the chat friendly and clean for everyone.

03

No Unauthorized Advertising

Promoting other servers, Discords, or external links without direct staff permission is strictly prohibited.

04

Protect Personal Privacy

Do not share sensitive personal information (phone numbers, real-life pictures, addresses, etc.) of yourself or others.

05

Staff Authority

Moderator and Admin decisions are final. Respect staff instructions and do not argue about timeouts or punishments in public chat.
